# OSLRS Enumerator Field Briefing

**Print this. Read it before your first day. Keep it in the bag.**

Version 2026-08-07 · Oyo State Labour & Skills Registry

---

## 1. The registration number — the thing people will ask you about

Every person you register gets an **OSLRS number** that looks like `OSL-2026-ABC123`. It is how
they prove they are on the register.

> ### ⚠️ DO NOT READ OUT A NUMBER UNTIL THE APP SHOWS YOU ONE
>
> When you finish a survey, the completion screen shows the number **only once it has uploaded**.
> If it says **"Not issued yet — this entry has not finished uploading"**, then **no number exists
> yet**. Do not invent one, do not read one from an earlier screen.
>
> Telling someone a number that does not exist is worse than telling them nothing. They will only
> find out when they try to use it, months later, and they will have no way to prove what you said.

**So what do you tell them?**

> *"Your registration is saved. Your number is being issued now — I will give it to you before I
> leave / I will send it to you today."*

**Then get the number from the Sync Status page.**

## 2. Sync Status — where the numbers actually live

Open **Sync Status** from the enumerator menu. Every survey you have taken is listed with:

- the **respondent's name**
- their **`OSL-2026-…` number** once uploaded, marked *"give this to the respondent"*
- or **"No number yet — not uploaded"** if it is still waiting

**Make this a habit: once you have signal, open Sync Status and write each number onto that
person's slip.** Nothing reminds you to do this. It is on you.

If the person gave an **email address**, they also receive their number by email automatically —
you do not need to do anything for them.

**If they gave no email, you are the only way they will ever learn their number.**

## 3. Households sharing one phone — read the number back, always

Several people in one compound often share a handset. That is normal and the system expects it:
**each person gets their own record**, even on the same phone.

But it has one consequence you must know:

> **A person on a shared phone CANNOT look themselves up by phone number later.** The system
> refuses to guess which of them is which — correctly, because guessing would show one person
> another person's registration.

**So for anyone on a shared handset, their OSLRS number is the ONLY way back to their record.**
Write it on their slip. Say it out loud. Make sure they have it somewhere they will not lose.

## 4. Discarding an interview

If a respondent changes their mind, or you realise it is the wrong person, or you have mis-entered
a name badly — use **"Discard this interview"** at the bottom of the form.

- It works at **any point** in the form
- It **deletes everything** entered — this cannot be undone
- It **submits nothing**; no number is issued

Use it. **Do not** carry on filling a form the person has withdrawn from, and **do not** leave a
half-finished form sitting on the device — the next respondent needs a clean start.

## 5. If a survey fails to upload

Open **Sync Status**. If an entry is marked as rejected:

- **"Reopen"** puts it back in your drafts so you can fix what is missing and send it again.
  **Use this first** — the answers are kept.
- **"Discard"** deletes it permanently. The person would have to be interviewed again from scratch.

**Always try Reopen before Discard.**

## 6. Questions people ask

**"Do I need my NIN?"**
No. They can register without it and add it later — the system will email them a link. Use
**"I don't have my NIN now"**. Never turn someone away for not having their NIN.

**"Is my information safe?"**
Yes. It is held by the Oyo State registry. They choose whether to appear in the Skills Marketplace,
and they can decline and still be registered.

**"Can I change my answers later?"**
Yes, using their OSLRS number at **oyoskills.com** → check registration.

## 7. Before you go out — checklist

- [ ] Phone charged, and a way to charge it in the field
- [ ] Logged in and the app opens **without** asking you to log in again
- [ ] You have completed **one practice survey** and seen where the number appears
- [ ] Slips or notebook for writing numbers onto
- [ ] You know how to reach your supervisor if something looks wrong

## For supervisors

**Never use real people's details to test.** If you need to try the app, use obviously fake names
and phone numbers, and tell the office so the rows can be removed. Test entries left in the register
become real-looking records that nobody can tell apart from citizens.

**Watch for:** enumerators reading out numbers before they exist (§1), and entries sitting unsynced
for days — those are people who have not been told their number.
